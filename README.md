<h3 id="project1">Project 1: RoadGuard AI</h3>
<p>
  RoadGuard AI is an AI-powered vehicle assistance and insurance claim support platform developed using Generative AI technologies. The system helps users diagnose vehicle breakdowns by analyzing uploaded images and provides appropriate guidance, including repair instructions, nearby garages, or towing assistance based on the severity of the issue. It also includes an intelligent insurance claim module that analyzes accident images and policy documents using AI agents to generate optimized claim reports and recommendations. The project leverages Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Semantic Search, and multi-agent workflows to deliver accurate and context-aware assistance.
